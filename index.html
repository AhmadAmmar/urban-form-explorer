Read a neighbourhood from its map

Pan to a place, zoom in until the button is ready, and press Analyse this view. The streets, buildings and places mapped in OpenStreetMap for that area are fetched live and measured in your browser: how the streets connect and orient, how densely and how tall the area is built, how near everyday needs are on foot, and how it serves cycling and public transport.

Nothing is stored and no account is needed. Each figure links to its definition on the methods page, and the guide teaches the same measures step by step in a notebook.

Map key after analysis

  • streets within 400 m walk of the centre, then 800, 1,200 m and beyond
  • buildings shaded by floors, light to dark; grey where floors are not mapped
  • shops, services, schools, parks and stops (click for names)
  • thicker lines: cycle lanes and tracks
